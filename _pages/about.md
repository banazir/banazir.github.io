I am a tenured Professor of Computer Science and Director of the **Knowledge Discovery in Databases (KDD) Laboratory** at Kansas State University. With over 25 years of experience in **Machine Learning**, **Predictive Analytics**, and **Cyber-Physical Systems**, I lead research on applying AI to complex problems in bioinformatics, robotics, and cyber-defense.

In addition to my academic work, I serve as a technical **Expert Witness** and consultant specializing in **AI Forensics** (deepfake detection, multimedia authentication) and **Data Governance**.

### Current Roles
* **Professor & Director:** KDD Laboratory, Kansas State University (1999–Present)
* **Principal Consultant:** Expert Witness Services (AI Forensics & Validity)
* **Co-PI:** U.S. Department of Agriculture (Computer Vision for Pollinator and Plant Phenotyping)
* **Co-PI:** U.S. Department of Defense (Foundation Models for Explainable, Multimodal Information Extraction)
  
### Recent Roles
* **SDRD PI:** Sandia National Laboratories (Autonomy for Hypersonics)

### Highlights
* **Award:** Recipient of the 2023 *Professorial Performance Award* and the 2018 *University Distinguished Faculty Award for Mentoring*.
* **Innovation:** ML Co-PI for the USDA-funded **BeeMachine** project and co-author of **pLM4ACE**.
* **Leadership:** Mentored 16 Ph.D. and 30+ M.S. graduates to positions at Meta, Microsoft, and Oak Ridge National Lab.
